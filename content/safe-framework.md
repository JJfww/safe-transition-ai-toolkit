# [SAFE Transition Framework](https://www.edsafeai.org/safe)

Status: draft

The [SAFE Framework](https://www.edsafeai.org/safe) adapts a responsible AI lens to transition education so staff can slow down, ask better questions, and keep decision-making anchored in people rather than automation.

## What this page helps staff do

This page gives staff a quick review structure for judging whether an AI use is acceptable, too risky, or not yet ready. It is meant to support discussion before use and reflection after a tool produces output.

## Safety

Transition-focused question: Are we protecting student data, privacy, and dignity?

- Practical review focus: what information is being shared, where it is going, and whether the student would be respected by the way the task is framed
- Warning sign: staff are pasting identifying information because it feels efficient

## Accountability

Transition-focused question: Who reviews the output and owns the final decision?

- Practical review focus: who is checking the output and who is responsible if it is wrong
- Warning sign: the output is treated as final because it sounds polished

## Fairness and Transparency

Transition-focused question: Does this reduce access, reinforce assumptions, or hide how decisions are made?

- Practical review focus: whether the tool is reinforcing narrow expectations about disability, work, independence, or readiness
- Warning sign: the output sounds neutral while quietly narrowing options or expectations

## Efficacy

Transition-focused question: Does this improve planning, instruction, communication, or student outcomes?

- Practical review focus: whether the AI use actually saves time or improves clarity without creating new confusion or risk
- Warning sign: the task feels impressive but does not make instruction, planning, or communication better
