# AI Drafting Lab for Transition and Special Education Work

Status: draft

## Privacy-screen boundary

The lab is the next step when staff are considering a careful, approved use. Its client-side privacy screen is a conservative teaching and review aid. It looks for likely identifying details and blocks prompt generation until the text appears adequately de-identified. It is not a FERPA compliance decision and does not make a sensitive use automatically appropriate.

Use the minimum information needed. If a district has approved a specific AI tool for this work, staff must follow the tool's data-use rules and district privacy guidance. District approval is not blanket permission to paste every detail from a student record.
