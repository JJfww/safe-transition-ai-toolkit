# Home

Status: draft

## Core message

The SAFE AI Toolkit is a practical, staff-facing resource for transition and special education educators, job coaches, paraprofessionals, and program leaders who support young adults with disabilities. It is designed to help teams use AI in ways that are useful, safe, student-centered, and grounded in human judgment.

## What this page helps staff do

This page gives staff a quick orientation to the toolkit's purpose, boundaries, and major sections. It should help a first-time visitor understand that the project is about transition-specific decision support and instructional planning, not about replacing human judgment with automation.

## What this toolkit is

- A transition-specific resource rather than a general AI-for-school guide
- A place to find practical guardrails, examples, prompt starters, and review tools
- A framework for using AI to support employment readiness, planning, communication, and instruction
- A starting point for staff who want to think carefully about privacy, dignity, and realistic use cases before copying anything into a tool

## What this toolkit is not

- A tool that makes decisions about students
- A replacement for student voice or professional judgment
- A live AI product that collects or sends user-entered text
- A shortcut around IEP team discussion, individualized planning, or educator review

## What still requires human review

- Whether the AI use fits the real student, setting, and workplace context
- Whether the output is accurate, respectful, and free of bias or overgeneralization
- Whether the language supports student voice rather than speaking for the student
- Whether the tool being used is actually approved for the situation

## Information staff should not enter casually

- Student names or direct identifiers
- Disability-related identifying details
- IEP information or protected educational records
- Family-private information
- Employer-sensitive information that should not be shared externally

## Signature feature

The AI Drafting Lab for Transition and Special Education Work helps staff prepare structured prompts for IEP development, transition plan development, and hidden curriculum work. Its privacy screen flags likely identifying or protected details before a prompt can be generated. This site itself does not send text to a model. It creates a structured prompt that users may choose to copy into an approved external AI tool after privacy review.
