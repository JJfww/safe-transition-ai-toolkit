About

This capstone is about responsible AI in transition education, not AI adoption for its own sake.

Transition work is individualized, future-facing, and deeply connected to student voice, access, and dignity. That makes it a strong place to ask harder questions about how AI should and should not be used.

What this page helps staff do

Understand why the toolkit is specific, cautious, and practical rather than broad, flashy, or product-driven.

What still requires human review

Whether the public language stays grounded in real transition practice and whether any example overstates what AI can responsibly do.

What “static site” means

This website is not an AI tool. Nothing you type here is sent to Gemini, ChatGPT, or another AI service. The lab runs in your browser, does not save your text, and creates a structured prompt only. An outside AI tool is involved only if you choose to copy that prompt and paste it there.

Why the AI Drafting Lab matters

The lab gives staff a structured way to prepare prompts for IEP development, transition plan development, and hidden curriculum work. It supports clearer drafting while keeping professional judgment with the people who know the student and context.

About the author

Jennifer Burnes, PhD

Jennifer Burnes, PhD, is a transition specialist, educator, and problem solver with more than 20 years of experience in instruction, research, program evaluation, and data-informed practice.

Her work focuses on finding practical solutions that make systems more useful for educators and expand access, independence, and opportunity for young adults with disabilities.

She provides education and technical assistance to adult learners, educators, job coaches, and school teams, translating complex information into usable tools, training, and support.

One of her proudest teaching moments came when a graduate student shared that, for the first time, she could explain complicated assessment information to a student's parents with confidence and accuracy rather than simply glossing over it.
